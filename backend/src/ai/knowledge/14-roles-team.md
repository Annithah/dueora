# Who can see and do what?

**Admin**: everything, including the team, companies and workspace settings. **Finance**: bills, payments, budgets, imports and reports. **Viewer**: can look but change nothing, and still receives reminders. An admin can give someone access to **all companies** or only to chosen ones, with a different role in each (for example Finance in one company and Viewer in another). People only see, and are only reminded about, the companies they have access to. Invite people from More → Team & roles; they get an email link to join.
