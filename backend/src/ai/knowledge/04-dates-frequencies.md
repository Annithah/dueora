# How are due dates and frequencies worked out?

Choose Weekly, Every 2 weeks, Monthly, Every 2 months, Quarterly, Every 6 months, Yearly, Custom (any number of weeks, months or years) or One-time. DueOra counts forward from the start date to the next due date, handles month-ends (a bill on the 31st falls on the 28th or 29th in February, then back to the 31st) and leap years, and moves the date forward by one cycle each time a cycle is fully paid. Dates use your workspace's timezone.
