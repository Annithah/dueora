# How do I add or edit a bill?

Tap **+** in the middle of the tab bar. Give it a name, choose the company and category, enter the amount in US dollars, pick how often it renews, and choose your reminders. The start date is today unless you change it, and the next due date is calculated for you (tap it to set your own). To edit, open the bill and tap **Edit**. Archive a bill to stop its reminders but keep its history.
