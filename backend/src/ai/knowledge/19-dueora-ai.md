# What can DueOra AI do?

Ask questions like "What's due this week?", "How much do we spend per year on software?" or "Compare this month with last month", or say what you want done: "Add internet, $50 every month, due on the 5th" or "We paid the hosting today by mobile money". DueOra AI always shows a confirmation card and only makes a change after you tap **Confirm**. It only sees your own workspace, never account references. Teach it facts in Settings → DueOra AI → What the AI knows. Admins can switch it off for the workspace.
