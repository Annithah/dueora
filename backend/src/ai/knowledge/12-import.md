# Can I import my existing spreadsheet?

Yes: More → Import from spreadsheet. Download the template (Excel or CSV) or use your own sheet with columns for company, category, name, provider, amount, frequency and next due date. Pick the file, match the columns, check the preview (errors are shown per row), then import. New companies and categories are created for you, and an empty category is guessed from the name. Admins and finance can import.
