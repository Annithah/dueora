# What do Upcoming, Outstanding and Overdue mean?

The Bills tab has segments: **Upcoming** (the next 7, 30, 60 or 90 days), **This month** (total, paid and left), **Next month**, **Overdue** (past the due date, with how many days late), **Outstanding** (everything due and not paid yet), **Paid** and **All**. If a monthly bill was missed for several months, each missed month counts — paying the oldest one moves on to the next. Each row shows the next reminder, and variable bills show "estimated" until the real amount is set.
