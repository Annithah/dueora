# What are companies and categories?

A workspace can hold several **companies**, for example the different businesses or branches you pay for. Every bill belongs to one company. Admins add, edit and archive companies in More → Companies; each can have its own initials, colour, description, reminder defaults, reminder time and monthly budget. A company with bills can't be deleted — archive it to hide it and keep its history. Use the company switcher at the top of Home, Bills, Calendar and Reports to look at one company or all of them.

**Categories belong to a company.** A new company starts empty, with categories you pick from the suggestions, or with a copy of another company's categories. A category can have **sub-categories** (one level, e.g. Utilities → Electricity, Water), a colour and icon, a description and defaults for new bills (how often, fixed or variable amount, reminders, payment method). Reorder, archive or merge categories in More → Categories; deleting one that has bills asks where to move them first.
