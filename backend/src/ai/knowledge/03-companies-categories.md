# What are companies and categories?

A workspace can hold several **companies**, for example the different businesses or branches you pay for. Every bill belongs to one company. Admins add, edit and archive companies in More → Companies; each one can have its own initials, colour, description, reminder defaults and monthly budget. A company with bills can't be deleted — archive it to hide it and keep its history. Use the company switcher at the top of Home, Bills, Calendar and Reports to look at one company or all of them. **Categories** group bills by type (Technology & software, Hosting & domains, Utilities and so on). DueOra has 11 built-in categories and you can add your own with a colourful icon in More → Categories.
