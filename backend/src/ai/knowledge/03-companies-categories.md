# What are companies and categories?

A workspace can hold several **companies**, for example the different businesses or branches you pay for. Every subscription belongs to one company; choose "+ New company" in the form to add one. **Categories** group subscriptions by type (Technology & software, Hosting & domains, Utilities and so on). DueOra has 11 built-in categories and you can add your own with a colourful icon in More → Categories.
