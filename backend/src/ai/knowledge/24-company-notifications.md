# Can I get notifications for only some companies?

Yes. In Settings → Notifications, open **Companies** and switch reminders, activity (payments, price changes) or summaries off for any company you don't want to hear about — or mute it completely. Each company can also have its own reminder time (More → Companies → Settings). On the 25th DueOra sends a preview of next month, on the 1st it tells you last month's report is ready, and the Monday digest lists what is still owed per company. You only ever hear about companies you have access to.
