# When do reminders go out?

At your workspace's reminder time (09:00 by default, in the workspace timezone): by default 30, 14 and 7 days, 3 days and 1 day before, on the due date, and 1, 3 and 7 days after, then weekly until it's paid or archived. Choose different reminders per subscription, or change the workspace defaults in Settings → Notifications. Reminders stop the moment a cycle is paid. If you would get more than 3 in one go, you get one grouped message instead.
