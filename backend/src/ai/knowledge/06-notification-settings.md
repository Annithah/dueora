# How do I control notifications?

Settings → Notifications lets you turn push, email and in-app alerts on or off, group busy days into one message, and choose the weekly digest (Mondays), the monthly summary (the 1st), budget alerts and team activity. Admins set the daily reminder time, quiet hours (messages wait until they end) and the default reminders. Use **Send test notification** to check push, email and in-app at once. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
