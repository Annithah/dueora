# How do I mute or snooze reminders?

Open the bill and tap •••. **Snooze** pauses its reminders just for you until tomorrow, in 3 days or next week; they come back when the snooze ends. **Mute** stops that bill's reminders for you until you unmute it. **Archive** (admins and finance) stops reminders for everyone and keeps the history. You can also tap Snooze on a reminder notification.
