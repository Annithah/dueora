# How do variable amounts work (electricity, water…)?

When you add the bill, choose **Variable amount** and enter an estimate. Each cycle shows "estimated" until you set the real amount: open the bill and tap **Set this cycle's amount**, or record the payment and switch on **Paid in full** — the cycle closes at what you paid. DueOra reminds the admins and finance members 5 days before the due date if the real amount isn't set yet. Past cycles always keep their own amounts, so history stays exact when the estimate changes.
