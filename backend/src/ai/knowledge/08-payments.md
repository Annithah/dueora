# How do I record a payment?

Open the bill and tap **Record payment** (or **Mark as paid** on a reminder notification). Choose full or partial, the date you paid and the method; it starts with the bill's usual method. A fully paid cycle moves the next due date forward; a one-time bill becomes completed. A partial payment keeps the cycle open and reminders mention what's left. Admins and finance can edit or delete a payment; the history is never lost.
