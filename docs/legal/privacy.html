Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

DueOra helps teams track bills and renewals and reminds them before payments are due. This page explains what we store, why, and the choices you have.

What we store

  • Your account: name, email address, a hashed password (never the password itself), language and timezone.
  • Your workspace: companies, categories, bills, amounts (in USD), due dates, payments and notes that you or your team enter.
  • Bill reference / account numbers: encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM), shown masked in the app, readable only by Admin and Finance members, never written to logs and never sent to the AI assistant.
  • Devices: a push notification token for each phone where you allow notifications.
  • Activity: an audit log of important changes (for example, who recorded a payment), and notification delivery records so you never get the same reminder twice.

DueOra has no ads and does not sell or share your data for marketing.

Who can see your data

Only the members of your workspace, and only the companies an admin gives them access to. Viewers can read but not change anything.

Services we use

  • Hosting for the DueOra server and database.
  • Email delivery for reminders, invitations and password reset codes.
  • Expo and Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver push notifications to your phone.
  • Google Gemini for the optional DueOra AI assistant. Admins can switch it off for the workspace. It only receives the bill details needed to answer a question, limited to the companies you can access, and never reference/account numbers.
  • Error reporting (when enabled) receives technical error details only, with request bodies, headers and user details removed.

Your choices

  • Turn notifications on or off per channel and per company in Settings → Notifications.
  • Export your data (Excel or JSON) in Settings → Data & privacy.
  • Delete your account in Settings → Data & privacy. Your personal details are removed; bills stay with the workspace they belong to.
  • Sign out of every device in Settings → Security.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Passwords are hashed, sign-in is rate limited, and sessions use short-lived tokens.

Contact

Questions or requests: use Settings → Help → Send feedback in the app.
